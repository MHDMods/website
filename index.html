About RB3 Pixel

RB3 Pixel is a leading educational platform dedicated to Computer Science and Internet Research. We provide students, researchers, and educators with specialized tools to explore the digital landscape in a safe, guaranteed, and isolated environment.

Our mission is to democratize access to advanced web analysis tools. Unlike standard browsing, our Educational Research Browser offers a controlled viewport into the web, allowing students to inspect network traffic, analyze web security headers, and understand the fundamental architecture of the modern internet without exposing their local networks to risk.

Core Educational Features

🔒 Safe Research Environment

Isolated containers ensure that students can study malware behavior, phishing patterns, and web exploits without user risk. Perfect for cybersecurity curriculum.

⏱️ Transient Sessions

Every research session is ephemeral. Data is automatically sanitized upon termination, teaching the principles of digital footprint management and privacy.

📂 Asset Management

Integrated tools for managing downloads and research assets, streamlining the data collection process for academic papers and research projects.

🖼️ High-Fidelity Rendering

Pixel-perfect rendering ensures that UI/UX students see exactly what is intended, supporting design capability studies across different viewports.
